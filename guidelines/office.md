# Office and presentation translation

## Typography

| Role | Web | Office-safe fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Display and headings | Fraunces | Cambria, then Georgia |
| Body | Inter | Calibri, then Arial |
| Labels and metadata | JetBrains Mono | Consolas |

Use mono type for labels, metadata, reference IDs, captions, and section
markers—not paragraph text.

## Word and PDF

- US Letter portrait unless the assignment requires another format.
- Use restrained paper surfaces, hairline rules, and generous margins.
- Use the canonical document header, metadata band, numbered heading, table,
  form prompt, signature block, and footer components.
- Repeat table headers across pages.
- Keep metadata bands, headings, and signature groups together.
- Use dark full-page covers selectively; body pages remain paper-first.
- Preserve written status labels so meaning never depends on color.

## PowerPoint

- Standard widescreen: `13.333 × 7.5 inches`.
- Safe area: at least `0.6 inches` horizontally and `0.45 inches` vertically.
- Deck title: at least `50 pt`.
- Slide title: at least `35 pt`.
- Mid-level heading: at least `24 pt`.
- Body: at least `16 pt`.
- One primary claim per slide.
- Prefer flat editorial composition to card grids or simulated application UI.
- Use dark and paper slides as deliberate pacing, not decoration.
- Keep page numbers, source notes, and footer treatment consistent.
- Provide sources in speaker notes for external visuals and nontrivial claims.
