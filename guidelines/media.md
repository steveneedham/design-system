# Media and photography

## Editorial direction

Use real documentary photography: on-location, candid, restrained, and useful
as evidence. Favor Columbus streets, civic spaces, mobility infrastructure,
field work, teams, and operational conditions. Natural light, dusk, golden
hour, and blue hour are preferred to studio lighting or generic stock imagery.

Public-facing mobility work remains organization-neutral unless a specific
organization is part of the assignment.

## Hero motion

- Use `16:9` or wider source media with a centered, resilient crop.
- Supply a representative poster image for every video.
- Apply `--media-hero-opacity` and `--media-hero-scrim`; do not bake the scrim
  into the media.
- Keep meaningful text out of video and poster imagery.
- Autoplay video must be muted, looping, and `playsinline`.
- Never make motion necessary to understand the page.
- Under reduced motion, keep the poster visible and remove nonessential
  animation.
- Print replaces motion with the paper surface; video and scrim are omitted.

## Still images

- Default editorial aspect ratio: `3:2`.
- Default wide evidence ratio: `16:9`.
- Avoid decorative crops that remove the operational context.
- Captions state what is visible, where relevant, and why it matters.
- Credit externally sourced images.
- Alt text describes the information the image contributes; use an empty alt
  attribute only when the image is genuinely decorative.

## Social crops

Compose banner masters inside the platform safe area and retain generous space
around the primary read. Keep profile-image overlap zones free of essential
text. Raster exports are downstream derivatives of the canonical SVG master.
