# Brand architecture

## Primary personal identity

Use the Steven Needham route-and-hub mark for the portfolio, civic work,
educational reports, public case studies, and organization-neutral mobility
content.

Canonical files:

- `assets/logo.svg`
- `assets/icons/lockup-horizontal-dark.svg`
- `assets/icons/lockup-horizontal-light.svg`
- `assets/icons/icon-square-dark.svg`
- `assets/icons/icon-square-light.svg`

## Consulting identity

Use the `Urban Strategist` descriptor and angled route/path mark for consulting
proposals, agreements, client communications, capability statements, kickoff
materials, and status reporting.

Canonical source masters:

- `assets/consulting/urban-strategist-lockups.svg`
- `assets/consulting/favicon.svg`
- `assets/consulting/linkedin-banner.svg`
- `assets/consulting/business-card.svg`
- `assets/consulting/email-signature.html`

These are editable masters. Downstream repositories may hold rendered exports,
but should not alter colors, type, geometry, or mark construction directly.

## Shared rules

- Amber represents operations and leadership.
- Teal represents systems, tools, and technical work.
- Use the matching light or dark asset for its intended surface.
- Preserve clear space equal to the diameter of the largest hollow node.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add shadows, or combine the marks.
- Public mobility work remains organization-neutral unless a specific
  organization is part of the assignment.
